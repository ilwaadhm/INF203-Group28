=== A3: SPARQL-spørring ===

Spørsmål: Hva er ankomsttiden og antall vogner for togturer som ender på Stabekk?

--- Spørring ---

PREFIX ex:   <https://home.bawue.de/~horsch/teaching/inf230/lab/tbox#>
PREFIX lab:  <https://home.bawue.de/~horsch/teaching/inf230/lab/>

SELECT ?time (COUNT(?coach) AS ?coaches)
WHERE {
  ?tur a ex:TrainJourney ;
       ex:hasEndStation lab:Stabekk ;
       ex:arrivalTime   ?time ;
       ex:operatedBy    ?tog .
  ?tog ex:hasCoach ?coach .
}
GROUP BY ?time
ORDER BY ?time

--- Resultat ---

?time                          ?coaches
--------------------------------------
2026-09-16T10:49:00            8
